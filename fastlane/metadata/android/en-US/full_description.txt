Keep track of important recurring dates without relying on external servers or accounts.

Features:
- Track fixed annual events such as birthdays, anniversaries, memorials, graduations, and milestones.
- Schedule local advance reminders on the event day or 1, 3, or 7 days beforehand.
- Customize categories, icons, colors, and light or dark appearance.
- Export and import your events as CSV files for backup or transfer.
- Available in English, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), and Spanish.
